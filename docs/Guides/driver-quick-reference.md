# Driver Quick Reference Guide
**Driver Assignment Overhaul - Quick Reference**

## New Notification Formats

### Segment-Based Notifications
- **Pickup**: "Pickup segment assigned - [Patient Name] from [Location] at [Time]"
- **Dropoff**: "Dropoff segment assigned - [Patient Name] to [Location] at [Time]"
- **Multi-Leg**: "Multi-leg assignment - Pickup → Dropoff → Travel Next"
- **Reassignment**: "Segment reassigned - Previous assignment cancelled"

### Enhanced Message Details
- **Route Information**: Clear pickup and dropoff locations
- **Timing**: Precise start and end times
- **Patient Context**: Patient name and appointment details
- **Special Instructions**: Any special requirements or notes

## Understanding Your Schedule

### Driver Lanes View
- **Your Lane**: Vertical timeline showing all your assigned segments
- **Segment Cards**: Color-coded by type (pickup, dropoff, travel, etc.)
- **Timing**: Clear start and end times for each segment
- **Status Updates**: Real-time status changes (en route, arrived, complete)

### Segment Types
- **🚗 Pickup**: Collecting patient from location
- **⬇ Dropoff**: Delivering patient to destination
- **↗ Travel Next**: Moving to next assignment
- **📍 Custom**: Specialized transportation needs
- **🚇 Metro Assist**: Helping with public transportation

## Status Updates

### Required Status Updates
1. **En Route**: When leaving for pickup location
2. **Arrived**: When reaching pickup/dropoff location
3. **Complete**: When segment is finished
4. **Delayed**: If running behind schedule

### How to Update Status
- **Via App**: Use status buttons in driver interface
- **Via Board**: Click segment card and select status
- **Via Phone**: Call dispatcher for manual updates

## Multi-Leg Assignments

### Understanding Multi-Leg Routes
- **Pickup → Dropoff**: Standard single patient transport
- **Pickup → Dropoff → Travel Next**: Multiple segments in sequence
- **Stay-with-Staff**: Remaining with patient during appointment
- **Handoff**: Transferring patient to another driver

### Route Planning
- **Travel Time**: System calculates time between segments
- **Buffer Time**: Built-in buffer for unexpected delays
- **Conflict Warnings**: Alerts for overlapping assignments
- **Optimization**: System suggests most efficient routes

## Communication with Dispatchers

### When to Contact Dispatcher
- **Delays**: Running behind schedule
- **Issues**: Problems with patient or location
- **Changes**: Patient requests or appointment modifications
- **Emergencies**: Safety concerns or urgent situations

### How to Contact
- **Phone**: Direct dispatcher contact number
- **App**: In-app messaging system
- **Emergency**: Emergency contact for urgent issues

## New Features for Drivers

### Real-Time Updates
- **Live Schedule**: Real-time updates to your assignments
- **Route Changes**: Automatic notifications of schedule modifications
- **Patient Updates**: Changes to patient information or requirements
- **System Alerts**: Important system-wide notifications

### Enhanced Information
- **Patient Details**: More comprehensive patient information
- **Location Details**: Enhanced pickup/dropoff location data

## Escalation Awareness

### Understanding Escalations
- **What**: Segments that need urgent driver assignment
- **When**: 6 hours before planned start time
- **Impact**: May affect your schedule if you're assigned
- **Response**: Be available for last-minute assignments

### Your Role
- **Availability**: Keep dispatcher informed of your availability
- **Flexibility**: Be prepared for schedule changes
- **Communication**: Respond quickly to urgent requests
- **Professionalism**: Maintain high service standards

## Performance Tracking

### Metrics You Can See
- **Completion Rate**: Percentage of segments completed on time
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Patient feedback scores
- **Efficiency**: Travel time vs. estimated time
- **Reliability**: Consistency in meeting schedules

### How to Improve
- **Punctuality**: Arrive on time for all segments
- **Communication**: Keep dispatcher informed of status
- **Professionalism**: Maintain high service standards
- **Flexibility**: Be available for additional assignments

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues
- **Notification Not Received**: Check app settings and connectivity
- **Schedule Not Updated**: Refresh app or contact dispatcher
- **Location Issues**: Verify address and contact dispatcher
- **Patient No-Show**: Follow no-show protocol

### Quick Solutions
- **App Issues**: Force close and reopen app
- **Connectivity**: Check internet connection
- **Location Problems**: Use GPS and contact dispatcher
- **Schedule Conflicts**: Contact dispatcher immediately

## Best Practices

### Daily Preparation
- **Check Schedule**: Review assignments for the day
- **Plan Routes**: Optimize travel between segments
- **Vehicle Check**: Ensure vehicle is ready and clean
- **Communication**: Confirm availability with dispatcher

### During Assignments
- **Arrive Early**: Allow buffer time for each segment
- **Update Status**: Keep dispatcher informed of progress
- **Professional Service**: Maintain high standards
- **Document Issues**: Report any problems or concerns

### End of Day
- **Complete All Segments**: Ensure all assignments are finished
- **Update Status**: Mark all segments as complete
- **Report Issues**: Document any problems or concerns
- **Plan Tomorrow**: Review next day's schedule

## Emergency Procedures

### Safety First
- **Emergency Contact**: Know emergency contact numbers
- **Safety Protocols**: Follow established safety procedures
- **Incident Reporting**: Report all incidents immediately
- **Patient Safety**: Prioritize patient well-being

### Communication
- **Emergency Line**: Use emergency contact for urgent issues
- **Dispatcher**: Contact dispatcher for non-emergency problems
- **Patient Care**: Ensure patient safety and comfort
- **Documentation**: Record all incidents and responses
